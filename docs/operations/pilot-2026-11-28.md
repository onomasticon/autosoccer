# The private pilot, 2026-11-28 (#101, gate 3)

Two runs of the new league through the production scheduler, job worker and rules (`scripts/phase2-pilot.ts`), each on an isolated local database with an accelerated clock:
- **an offline rehearsal**, with every model replaced by the deterministic stand-ins;
- **a live run** with the 20 qualified models, capped at $50 of model spend.

Live and offline results are kept apart. The offline numbers say nothing about how the models behave.

## The offline rehearsal: the whole pipeline, two seasons

- **Season 1:** founding, the talent pool, three recruitment rounds, the safety net, then a 38-round season with both market windows.
- **Rollover:** [rollover/1](../platform/rollover.md) created season 2, retiring 9 players and signing 9 academy graduates. In season 2's preseason window, 161 contracts came up for renewal.
- **Season 2** played in full, and the league rolled on into season 3.
- **Checks:** no invariant failed, no fixture failed, and every round was played.
- **Money:** at offline prices, which only show the plumbing, the run cost $29.10 for 1.6 seasons. No club borrowed, and clubs ended season 1 with Ȼ13–19m.

## The live run: founding, recruitment and 14 rounds

### What it found, and what was fixed

1. **Market prompts were too big for the models that think at length.** Showing every club the whole pool of 300+ players made recruitment prompts of 25–85k tokens. Gemini 4 Neon, DeepSeek V4 Pro, GLM 5.3 and Qwen 3.8 Max ran out of answer room at 4,000 tokens, and Grok 4.7 and Kimi K3.1 Pro hit the 120 s timeout. That's why the safety net built most squads. **Fixed in #124:**
   - a shortlist (the best 10 and cheapest 5 at each position, the same for every club);
   - 12,000 tokens of answer room for contract decisions;
   - a 300 s timeout for every call.
2. **Pre-match answers were cut off too.** DeepSeek V4 Pro's thinking passed 4,000 tokens, so its side went unchanged. **Fixed in #125:** pre-match decisions get 12,000 tokens. Before the outage below, 27 of 245 manager calls failed (11%): 13 Kimi timeouts and 10 cut-off answers, both fixed now, plus 2 rate limits.
3. **The OpenRouter account has a daily budget of $10.** At 10:39 UTC it ran out, and the 14 models routed through OpenRouter were refused from then on. The direct models (OpenAI and Anthropic) kept answering. Production behaves well in this case: each refused club keeps its last valid decision, and every match is played. Two consequences:
   - **In real time this doesn't bind.** OpenRouter's share is about $1.19 a day; the pilot's accelerated clock compressed 14 rounds into two hours.
   - **The account limit must stay above the forecast,** or 14 clubs go dark every day. See [spend-forecast.md](spend-forecast.md).
4. **The meter was too conservative.** It booked each refused call at half its worst case, so it reached the $50 cap about 10 minutes after the outage began, on $13.44 of real spend. Refusals (an HTTP 4xx other than a timeout) now cost $0 in the meter.

## Not yet shown live

- **Distress live:** loans, defaults, auctions and administration with live managers. Nobody borrowed in 14 rounds. The tests and the harness exercise these.
- **Rollover with live models:** only the offline rehearsal reached it.
- **The fixes, on live models:** the effect of #124 and #125 needs a live run with OpenRouter available. A rerun is `scripts/phase2-pilot.ts` with a fresh database and `PILOT_CAP_USD`.
