# Economy surrogate vs the real simulator (integrated mode)

Generated by `bun run economy:integrated --seasons 2`: 2 season(s) of 10 clubs, 7-a-side, through the normal lifecycle (scheduler, offline managers, pinned simulator) on a throwaway database, production match length. 180 matches in 699 s.

Strength is the mean overall rating of each starting seven; the median gap between the sides was 0.43 points (90th percentile 1.71).

| | Matches | Goals per match | Draws | Home wins | Favourite wins (gap ≥ 1) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Real simulator | 180 | 2.79 | 21% | 35% | 28% |
| Surrogate, fitted | 9000 | 2.78 | 25% | 32% | 29% |
| Surrogate, current default | 9000 | 3.00 | 24% | 38% | 46% |

Fitted parameters (maximum likelihood): goals per side 1.50, home advantage -0.18, slope -0.100 per rating point. Current default: 1.50, 0.00, 0.060.

Identifiability: with a median strength gap of 0.43 points the slope is poorly determined; a fitted slope near 0 means "no detectable effect at these gaps", not "strength doesn't matter". The harness keeps an explicit slope assumption (see DEFAULT_MATCH).

Caveats: one small sample; the offline managers' tactics, not the LLMs'. The surrogate only sees squad strength, so tactical
edges (the simulator rewards some setups strongly, see docs/research/sim-plausibility) show up as unexplained variance.
## Findings and what the harness does with them

- **Goals: the surrogate now matches.** The default is 1.5 goals per side, about 3.0 a match against the simulator's 2.8–3.0 across both runs. The old default (1.6 goals per side, home advantage +0.08) overstated goals and home wins.
- **Away sides won more often.** In 180 real matches the away side won 44% and the home side 35%, so the fit lands on a negative home advantage. That's about two standard errors, which is suggestive, not conclusive. It may be a side bias in the simulator, or in how the offline managers set up away from home. The surrogate keeps **no** home advantage until a larger run, or a check in autosoccer-sim, settles it. Follow-up: rerun with `--seasons 6` and compare blue/red sides directly.
- **Strength doesn't predict results at these gaps.** With starting sevens a median 0.43 rating points apart, the fitted slope runs to the edge of the grid (−0.10). Mean rating isn't a useful predictor here: tactics and match noise dominate, as the sim-plausibility study found. The surrogate keeps its explicit +0.06 per point assumption, so its competition metrics (title concentration, mobility) are probably **less** random than the real league, and its forfeit and distress rates are unaffected. The economy's money flows depend on results only through win/draw income and prize places, so this mainly shifts who earns, not how much enters or leaves the league.
